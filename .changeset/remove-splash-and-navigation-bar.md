Remove `Splash` and `NavigationBar`.

The splash curtain and the top loading bar are out of margo-ui's scope and have no replacement in this package.

**Removed**

- the `Splash` component and `Splash.Serve`
- the `SplashProps` and `SplashServeProps` types
- the `margo-splash-idle` custom variant
- the `NavigationBar` component
- the `NavigationBarProps` type
